# ActionsManager.io Cloud Terms of Service Draft

Cloud/SaaS is not part of the first public self-hosted beta. This Terms of Service draft is retained for future hosted-service legal review only; it is not final legal text and does not describe an active paid or hosted beta offering. A qualified attorney should review final Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, subscription terms, disclaimers, and commercial terms before any hosted or paid launch.

## 1. Account Use

Users authenticate with GitHub OAuth and use ActionsManager.io to manage GitHub Actions workflows, projects, repositories, pull requests, and related metadata. You are responsible for activity under your account and for keeping GitHub access secure.

## 2. GitHub OAuth and GitHub Marketplace

ActionsManager.io integrates with GitHub OAuth for authentication and GitHub Marketplace for Cloud/SaaS subscription management. GitHub remains a separate service with its own terms, policies, availability, and billing systems.

## 3. Subscription Plans

Cloud/SaaS plans may include Free, Professional, and Enterprise tiers. Paid plan access is determined by the active GitHub Marketplace subscription and verified Marketplace webhooks.

## 4. Payment

Cloud/SaaS subscription payment is handled through GitHub Marketplace unless a separate written commercial agreement says otherwise. ActionsManager.io does not directly collect payment card details for Marketplace-managed subscriptions.

## 5. Acceptable Use

Do not use the service to abuse GitHub APIs, bypass rate limits, access repositories without authorization, upload malicious content, interfere with other tenants, attack the service, or violate applicable laws or third-party rights.

## 6. Service Availability

The Cloud/SaaS service may change, pause, or experience downtime for maintenance, updates, incidents, GitHub outages, infrastructure issues, or security needs. Any uptime commitments should appear only in a separate written agreement.

## 7. User Responsibilities

You are responsible for GitHub organization permissions, repository access, workflow changes, pull requests, OAuth authorization, account membership, and reviewing generated or edited workflow files before use.

## 8. Termination

Access may be suspended or terminated for non-payment, cancellation through GitHub Marketplace, security risk, violation of acceptable-use rules, or other reasons defined in the final Terms or a commercial agreement.

## 9. Disclaimers

Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, the service and documentation are provided as-is. ActionsManager.io does not guarantee that generated workflows, automation changes, or repository configurations will be error-free.

## 10. Limitation of Liability

Final limitation-of-liability language must be reviewed by counsel and included in the final Terms of Service or commercial agreement.

## 11. Privacy

The service processes account, repository, workflow, pull request, billing, and operational metadata to operate the SaaS. See [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for the current privacy-policy placeholder.

## 12. Attorney Review Required

This document is a product draft. Final Terms of Service language, privacy references, marketplace billing terms, warranty disclaimers, liability limits, governing law, and commercial terms must be reviewed by a qualified attorney before public launch.
