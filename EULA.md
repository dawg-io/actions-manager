# ActionsManager.io Self-Hosted EULA Draft

This draft is retained for future legal/commercial review and is not final legal text for the self-hosted beta. ActionsManager Self-Hosted is currently a free beta preview with no paid plans available. A qualified attorney should review final EULA, license terms, warranty language, limitations, and commercial terms before any paid or general-availability launch.

## 1. License Scope

The Community/Core source code is available under the Apache License 2.0. Paid Professional and Enterprise capabilities, packaged distributions, support, and license-key-enabled features may be provided under separate commercial terms.

## 2. Permitted Use

You may run ActionsManager.io in your own self-hosted environment for your internal repositories and workflows, subject to the tier and agreement that applies to your deployment.

## 3. Restrictions

You may not use the product to violate applicable law, interfere with GitHub or third-party services, bypass tier limits, share paid entitlements outside the permitted scope, or remove required notices from Apache 2.0 Community/Core materials.

## 4. License Keys

The Free tier does not require a license key. Professional and Enterprise tiers may require a valid license key. The license key controls access to paid features and tier limits; it does not grant ActionsManager.io ownership of customer repositories, workflows, secrets, or data.

Invalid, expired, missing, or tampered self-hosted license keys should fail closed to the Free tier.

## 5. Tier Limits

Tier limits may include project counts, repositories per project, secrets per project, support level, or other product capabilities. Published limits should be treated as product limits unless a written commercial agreement says otherwise.

## 6. Updates

Updates may include security fixes, bug fixes, feature changes, or tier-limit changes. You are responsible for testing and applying updates in your self-hosted environment unless a separate support agreement says otherwise.

## 7. Support Expectations

Community/Core use may rely on community support. Professional or Enterprise support, response targets, or service levels apply only if included in a separate commercial agreement.

## 8. Termination

Access to paid features may end if the license expires, is cancelled, is not paid, or is used outside the permitted scope. On termination, the deployment may continue operating at the Free tier where technically supported.

## 9. Warranty Disclaimer

Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, the software and documentation are provided as-is, without warranties of any kind.

## 10. Limitation of Liability

Any limitation of liability should be defined in the final commercial agreement or EULA reviewed by counsel. This draft does not set final liability terms.

## 11. Data Ownership

Customers retain ownership of their repositories, workflow files, pull requests, project metadata, and configuration data. License enforcement controls feature access only and does not transfer ownership of customer data.

## 12. Secrets and Sensitive Data

ActionsManager.io must not store GitHub repository secret values in plaintext. Operators are responsible for securing their own deployment, environment variables, database, backups, OAuth credentials, and GitHub tokens.

## 13. Commercial-Only and License Enforcement Features

Do not reverse engineer, bypass, disable, or tamper with commercial-only features, license-key checks, tier enforcement, or entitlement controls except where expressly allowed by applicable law or a written agreement.

## 14. Attorney Review Required

This document is a product draft. Final EULA language, jurisdiction, consumer-law terms, warranty disclaimers, liability limits, and commercial terms must be reviewed by a qualified attorney before public launch.
